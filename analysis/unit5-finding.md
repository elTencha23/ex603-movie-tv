# Unit 5 – Recommendations

**To:** Leadership team
**Subject:** What to do about the movies that fail often and the movies that never started

Our analysis found two different problems in the catalog. They need different responses: one group has activity that goes wrong, and the other has no activity at all.

## Group 1: Movies that fail often (movies 11, 9 and 2)

| Movie | Ratings | Withdrawn | Failure rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11 | 16 | 5 | 31% |
| 9 | 14 | 4 | 29% |
| 2 | 16 | 4 | 25% |

The platform average is 17%. Each of these movies also fails more often than every other movie from the same country, and they receive a normal number of ratings, so the problem is not low activity. They do not share a single genre either.

**Recommendation: review why their ratings are being withdrawn before taking action against the producers.**
- Go through each withdrawn rating's reason. For movie 11, 2 of its 5 failures were user requests, which are not the producer's fault.
- If spam or policy violations are the main cause, check whether the same few users are responsible. If so, act on those users, not the producer.
- Movie 9 is also currently unavailable. Confirm whether that is related to its failures.

## Group 2: Movies that never started (movies 14 and 15)

These two movies have **no ratings at all**, even though both are marked available and were added to the catalog in early 2022. Because they have no ratings, they never appear in failure reports, so this problem stays hidden unless someone looks for it.

**Recommendation: find out whether users can actually see these movies, before judging them.**
- Add genre tags. Neither movie has one, which may keep it out of search and recommendations.
- Check whether anyone has watched them. If people watch but never rate, the rating feature may be the problem.
- Set a review date. If the movies are visible and still get no activity after that, decide whether to keep them in the catalog.

## Summary

| Group | Movies | The problem | First action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fail often | 11, 9, 2 | Ratings are being withdrawn | Review the withdrawal reasons |
| Never started | 14, 15 | No ratings at all | Check that users can find them |

The data shows *what* is happening, but not *why*. Each recommendation starts with an investigation so that we act on causes, not guesses.
